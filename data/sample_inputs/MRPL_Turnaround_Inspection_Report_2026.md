### 2.0 Inspection Grid Readings & Anomaly Log

#### 2.1 Crude Distillation Unit (CDU-1) Transfer Line Pipe Section P-104B
- **Location:** Column C-101 overhead transfer spool to furnace F-101 (Grid 4B, Bend B3).
- **Material:** Carbon Steel ASTM A106 Gr B, 12" Sch 80 (Nominal: 8.00 mm).
- **Ultrasonic Thickness Measured:** **3.42 mm** residual wall thickness.
- **Visual Observations:** Severe internal localized naphthenic acid groove corrosion and deep pitting (pit depths up to 2.1 mm).
- **Design Retirement Minimum:** **4.80 mm** (as per MRPL SOP-17).
- **Status:** **CRITICAL SAFETY VIOLATION — NON-COMPLIANT**.

#### 2.2 VGO Hydrocracker High-Pressure Flange FL-208
- **Location:** Reactor R-201 inlet nozzle flange (Class 1500 RTJ).
- **Hydro-Test Pressure:** 142 bar.
- **Observation:** Surface micro-fissuring detected on ring joint gasket seating groove via fluorescent dye penetrant.
- **Status:** **HIGH SEVERITY — REQUIRES GASKET REPLACEMENT & RE-FACING**.

#### 2.3 DHT Heat Exchanger E-102 Shell
- **Location:** Diesel hydrotreater feed/effluent exchanger shell.
- **Measured Thickness:** 3.90 mm (Nominal: 4.50 mm, Minimum: 3.20 mm).
- **Status:** **COMPLIANT — SCHEDULE MONITORING NEXT TURNAROUND**.
