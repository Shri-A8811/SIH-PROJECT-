# MRPL STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE — SOP-04
## Title: High-Pressure Flanged Joint Integrity and Gasket Management
**Document No:** MRPL-MNT-SOP-004-REV3  
**Classification:** Confidential PSU Internal  
**Facility:** Mangalore Refinery & Petrochemicals Limited

---

### Section 1.0 Objective
Establishes mandatory torque, alignment, and non-destructive surface examination standards for ASME Class 900 and Class 1500 flanged joints across hydrocracker and high-pressure desulfurization units.

---

### Section 2.0 Ring Type Joint (RTJ) Flange Specifications
<!-- Page 2 -->
Class 1500 flanges (such as VGO Hydrocracker Flange FL-208) utilize octagonal metallic ring joint gaskets. Gasket seating grooves must maintain mirror-smooth surface finish free of radial scores or erosion grooves.

---

### Section 3.0 Hydrostatic Pressure Testing and Crack Acceptance Criteria
<!-- Page 3 -->
#### Section 3.1 Zero-Tolerance Crack Policy
During turnaround hydro-testing at 142 to 150 bar, flanged connections are subjected to 100% fluorescent dye penetrant testing (DPT).
- Any detected micro-fissuring, surface cracking, or radial groove scoring greater than 0.20 mm represents an immediate **HIGH SEVERITY INTEGRITY BREACH**.
- Gasket seating surfaces exhibiting micro-fissures require on-site flange re-facing and complete replacement with an API 6A certified spiral wound gasket prior to plant startup.
