# MRPL STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE — SOP-17
## Title: Pressure Vessel, Transfer Line, and Piping Integrity Standards
**Document No:** MRPL-ENG-SOP-017-REV4  
**Classification:** Confidential PSU Internal  
**Scope:** Mangalore Refinery & Petrochemicals Limited (Refinery Units 1, 2 & 3)

---

### Section 1.0 Purpose and Scope
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines the mandatory engineering inspection protocols, Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) intervals, and quantitative retirement thickness criteria for crude distillation transfer lines, high-temperature process piping, and pressure vessels.

---

### Section 2.0 Inspection Methodology and NDT Protocol
<!-- Page 2 -->
All primary crude transfer lines operating above 280°C shall undergo 100% Ultrasonic Thickness Gauging (UTG) at every scheduled major turnaround (minimum 5-year frequency). Inspection points must include all 90-degree elbows, reducer spools, and high-velocity impingement zones.

---

### Section 3.0 Corrosion Rates and Naphthenic Acid Attack
Naphthenic acid and high-temperature sulfidic corrosion accelerate metal loss in carbon steel spools. Where crude TAN (Total Acid Number) exceeds 1.5 mg KOH/g, corrosion rates exceeding 0.45 mm/year must trigger metallographic replication and corrosion coupon analysis.

---

### Section 4.0 Minimum Safe Retirement Thickness & Replacement Thresholds
<!-- Page 4 -->
#### Section 4.1 Nominal Design Margins
Standard Schedule 80 carbon steel transfer piping (e.g. Line P-104B) has a nominal design wall thickness of **8.00 mm** (with a standard corrosion allowance of 3.20 mm).

#### Section 4.2 Mandatory Retirement Thickness (Critical Safety Threshold)
Under no operating circumstances shall the residual wall thickness of any primary crude distillation transfer line be permitted to operate below the mandatory minimum retirement limit of **4.80 mm**.
- **Action on Breach:** If ultrasonic gauging reveals a residual wall thickness less than **4.80 mm** at any measured inspection grid, the spool is deemed **NON-COMPLIANT** and must be condemned immediately.
- **Replacement Mandate:** The affected pipe section must be de-inventoried and physically replaced with an emergency pre-fabricated spool prior to unit recommissioning. Operation under derated pressure is strictly prohibited for Class A process lines.

---

### Section 5.0 Weld Seam Inspection & Radiography
<!-- Page 5 -->
All replacement spools must undergo 100% radiographic testing (RT) of circumferential butt welds and hydrostatic proof testing at 1.5x maximum allowable working pressure (MAWP).
